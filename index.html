Our story

Dennis in the field.
Kathy in the office.

“Who's the D.K. in D.K. Boos? The answer is Dennis and Kathy.”

They built the shop on a hands-on approach. Today their son and his wife run it the same way: same family, same standards, and a crew of full-time installers who've been doing this a long time.

Nearly five decades in, we're still the shop South Sound homeowners, contractors and architects call when the glass has to be right.
