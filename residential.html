Good questions

Asked often.

Something else on your mind? Call the shop. A real person picks up.

Why should I replace my windows?
Better energy efficiency and lower utility bills, a big lift in curb appeal, and windows that open, close and clean easily again. If yours are drafty, foggy or hard to operate, it's worth a look.
When do I need a new entry door?
Drafts around the frame, a door that sticks or won't latch, visible rot or warping, or simply a front entry that doesn't match the house anymore. We'll tell you honestly whether repair or replacement makes more sense.
Can you fix a foggy window without replacing it?
Often, yes. Fog between panes means the seal on the insulated glass unit has failed. We can usually replace just that glass unit and keep your existing frame.
