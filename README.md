# D.K. Boos Glass — concept redesign

An unofficial, just-for-fun redesign concept for D.K. Boos Glass (Lacey, WA). Not affiliated with or endorsed by the company; the official site is [dkboosglass.com](https://www.dkboosglass.com/).

Single static page (`index.html`): no build step. Photos are from [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com). The estimate form is a non-functional demo.

## Pages

- `index.html` is the home page, with the logo intro and the Glass Lab
- `residential.html`, `shower-doors.html`, `commercial.html`, `skylights.html`, `specialty.html` and `rv-auto.html` are the service pages
- `about.html`, `faq.html` and `contact.html`
- `assets/site.css` and `assets/site.js` are shared by every page

The subpages and the shared nav and footer are generated by `tools/build.py`. Edit the content there, then run:

    python3 tools/build.py
